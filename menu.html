Food

Tabby Pancakes

Fluffy, golden pancakes stacked high and drizzled with warm maple syrup. Each “stripe” of cinnamon sugar gives the stack a cute tabby-cat look, making this breakfast as fun as it is delicious. Soft, cozy, and perfect for a sweet start to your café visit.

The Purrrfect Panini

Roasted turkey, basil pesto, tomato, and melted mozzarella pressed between warm ciabatta. Crispy outside, melty inside — like a cozy hug in sandwich form.

pawsome Potato bites

Crispy smashed potatoes roasted with herbs, sea salt, and pepper, served with a rich garlic aioli. Crunchy on the outside, fluffy inside — a simple, satisfying café snack.

Meowcarons

Adorable cat-shaped French macarons with crisp shells and soft, chewy centers. Offered in rotating flavors like strawberry cream, pistachio, and chocolate ganache, they’re both delicious and irresistibly cute.

whisker whip latte
